# SmartHire AI — HR Knowledge Base

This document contains the static knowledge base for the HR Assistant Agent. The agent uses this content to answer recruitment process questions grounded in factual policy. If a question falls outside this knowledge base, the agent must say it does not know and recommend escalation to human HR.

## Recruitment Process Overview

### Stages of Hiring at TalentBridge HR Solutions

1. **Application Submission** — Candidates apply through our careers portal or are referred by employees. All applications are acknowledged within 3 business days.

2. **Resume Screening** — The recruitment team reviews resumes against job requirements. Shortlisting is based on skills match, experience relevance, and education fit. This typically takes 3-5 business days.

3. **Initial Phone Screen** — A 20-30 minute phone call with the recruiter to verify basic qualifications, discuss salary expectations, and assess cultural fit. Scheduled within 1 week of shortlisting.

4. **Technical Interview** — A 45-60 minute technical assessment, which may include coding challenges, system design exercises, or portfolio reviews depending on the role. Scheduled within 1-2 weeks of phone screen.

5. **Behavioral Interview** — A 30-45 minute interview with the hiring manager focusing on soft skills, leadership, and team collaboration. May be combined with the technical interview for some roles.

6. **Final Decision** — The hiring team reviews all interview feedback and makes a decision within 5 business days of the final interview.

7. **Offer Stage** — Verbal offer followed by written offer letter within 2 business days. Candidates have 5 business days to accept or negotiate.

8. **Onboarding** — Pre-boarding begins immediately upon offer acceptance. Day 1 onboarding includes IT setup, team introductions, and policy orientation.

### Timeline Expectations

| Stage | Typical Duration |
|-------|-----------------|
| Application to Phone Screen | 1-2 weeks |
| Phone Screen to Technical Interview | 1-2 weeks |
| Technical to Behavioral Interview | 1 week |

## Candidate Status Definitions

- **Applied** — Application received and pending initial review.
- **Shortlisted** — Resume has been reviewed and meets basic requirements; candidate is advancing to phone screen.
- **Phone Screen Scheduled** — A phone screen has been scheduled with the candidate.
- **Phone Screen Complete** — Phone screen has been completed; pending decision on next steps.
- **Interview Scheduled** — Technical or behavioral interview has been scheduled.
- **Interviewed** — All required interviews have been completed; pending final decision.
- **Offer Extended** — A formal offer has been made to the candidate.
- **Offer Accepted** — Candidate has accepted the offer.
- **Offer Declined** — Candidate has declined the offer.
- **Rejected** — Candidate was not selected after evaluation. A courtesy email is sent within 3 business days.

## Common Candidate Questions

### "What is the status of my application?"
Applications typically move through the following stages: Applied → Shortlisted → Phone Screen → Interviews → Decision → Offer. You can check your application status through the candidate portal, or contact our recruitment team for updates. We aim to provide updates within 5 business days of any status change.

### "When will I hear back?"
After applying, you will receive an acknowledgment within 3 business days. If your application is shortlisted, you can expect to be contacted within 1-2 weeks for a phone screen. We understand the importance of timely communication and strive to keep candidates informed at every stage.

### "What should I prepare for the interview?"
- **Technical Interview**: Review the job description's required skills. Be prepared to discuss your past projects and demonstrate your technical abilities through coding exercises or system design discussions.
- **Behavioral Interview**: Use the STAR method (Situation, Task, Action, Result) to structure your responses. Prepare examples of teamwork, leadership, conflict resolution, and problem-solving.
- **General**: Research our company, review the job posting, and prepare thoughtful questions about the team and role.

### "Can I reapply if I was rejected?"
Yes, candidates may reapply after 6 months from the date of rejection. We encourage continued professional development during this period. Please note that previous application data will be retained per our data retention policy.

### "Do you offer visa sponsorship?"
Visa sponsorship is evaluated on a case-by-case basis depending on the role and candidate qualifications. Please discuss this during your phone screen with the recruiter.

### "What is the salary range?"
Salary ranges are shared during the phone screen stage and are competitive based on experience, skills, and market benchmarks. We also offer comprehensive benefits including health insurance, 401(k) matching, and professional development budgets.

## Recruiter Guidelines

### Scheduling Best Practices
- Schedule phone screens within 1 week of shortlisting.
- Schedule technical interviews within 1-2 weeks of phone screen completion.
- Always confirm availability with both the candidate and interviewer before booking.
- Send calendar invitations with meeting links and preparation instructions at least 24 hours in advance.

### Communication Standards
- Acknowledge applications within 3 business days.
- Provide status updates at least every 5 business days during active evaluation.
- Send rejection notifications within 3 business days of a decision.
- Always be professional, respectful, and empathetic in all candidate communications.

### Escalation Triggers
Escalate to HR management if:
- A candidate raises a formal complaint about the process.
- A candidate requests accommodations for disabilities or religious observances.
- There is a potential conflict of interest (e.g., candidate is a relative of an employee).
- A candidate asks legal questions about employment contracts or non-competes.
- Compensation negotiations exceed the approved band by more than 15%.

## Data Privacy and Compliance

- All candidate data is stored securely and accessed only by authorized recruitment personnel.
- Candidates have the right to request deletion of their data under GDPR/CCPA.
- Interview notes and evaluations are confidential and shared only with the hiring team.
- We do not share candidate information with third parties without explicit consent.
